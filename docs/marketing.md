# Support2Docs — Value Proposition & ROI

## The Problem

Support teams answer the same questions repeatedly, but rarely have time to document the solutions. Knowledge stays trapped in closed tickets instead of helping future customers.

---

## What Support2Docs Does

> **Automatically turns recurring support tickets into Knowledge Base articles — with a human approving before anything is published.**

1. **Customers submit support tickets** (Zendesk / Intercom) → tickets are sent to the system via webhook when closed
2. **AI detects patterns** — when 5+ tickets describe the same problem, they are grouped into a cluster
3. **GPT-4o writes a draft KB article** — extracts the problem, root cause, and solution from the ticket history
4. **A human reviews the draft** — edits if needed, then approves or discards
5. **Approved drafts are published** to your KB system (Notion, Confluence, or any webhook endpoint)

---

## ROI Breakdown

### Time saved on documentation
- Writing one KB article from scratch: **30–60 min**
- Reviewing and approving an AI-generated draft: **3–5 min**
- Publishing **10 articles/month** → saves **~8 hours/month per engineer**

### Deflected support tickets
- Every published KB article enables customer self-service
- Industry average: one good KB article deflects **50–200 tickets/year**
- At $15–25 cost per ticket handled → **$750–$5,000 saved per article per year**

### Faster agent onboarding
- New support agents search the KB instead of asking colleagues
- Reduces ramp-up time from weeks to days

### Knowledge retention
- When experienced agents leave, their solutions stay in the KB
- Prevents the same issues from being rediscovered repeatedly

## Who Benefits Most

| Company type | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| SaaS with small support teams | Can't afford dedicated technical writers |
| Fast-growing startups | Support volume grows faster than the team |
| Developer tools / APIs | Technical issues repeat and need accurate docs |
| E-commerce | High ticket volume, many recurring issues |

---

## Real-World Example

A company receives 200 tickets/month about the same password reset issue. Each ticket takes 8 minutes to handle — that's **26 hours/month** on one problem. One KB article eliminates most of them. Support2Docs drafts that article automatically the moment the 5th ticket comes in, and a reviewer approves it in under 5 minutes.

---

## What It Is Not

- Does **not** replace support agents
- Does **not** respond to customers automatically
- Does **not** publish without human approval — the reviewer is always in the loop
